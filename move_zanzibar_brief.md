# Move Zanzibar - Branding & Content Guide

This document contains the official branding guidelines and core website content for **Move Zanzibar**. Use this as the structural and stylistic foundation to build their official website.

---

## 🎨 1. Brand Identity & Visual System

### Color Palette
*   **Primary / Ocean Turquoise:** `#00A896` (Represents the crystal-clear waters of Jambiani beach, freshness, freedom, and dynamic movement).
*   **Secondary / Warm Orange:** `#FF6B35` (Represents the energy of acrobatic shows, Zanzibari sunsets, and community warmth).
*   **Neutral Dark / Deep Charcoal:** `#1A1A1A` (Used for text and high-contrast logo backgrounds, representing strength and discipline).
*   **Neutral Light / Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Used for clean backgrounds, clarity, and open spaces).

### Typography
*   **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** Sans-Serif Bold or Display Organic (e.g., *Montserrat*, *Cabinet Grotesk*, or a clean handwritten font style). Reflects raw energy, youthfulness, and community-driven power.
*   **Body Text:** Modern, highly legible Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Open Sans*). Ensures excellent readability for international volunteers and partners.

## 📝 2. Core Website Content & Copywriting

### Hero Section
*   **Tagline:** Empowering Youth Through Art & Movement
*   **Value Proposition:** We provide young talents in Zanzibar with the opportunity to develop their skills and build a better future.

---

### Who We Are
Move Zanzibar is a group of young artists, acrobats, and dancers. Our mission is to nurture talent, inspire creativity, and train the next generation for a brighter future.

---

### Core Pillars

#### 🎪 Performances
We create acrobatic and dance performances in resorts across Zanzibar. These shows support our community.

#### 🤸 Youth Program
Free training for children and teenagers focused on movement, discipline, and confidence.

#### 🌍 Community
A living community of artists who train, live, and grow together through movement.

---

### Our Impact
Every performance directly supports our community in Zanzibar. Through our work we provide:

*   **Free training** for children and young people.
*   **Daily support** for our community of artists.
*   **Access to equipment**, food, and safe training spaces.
*   **Opportunities for youth** to develop discipline and confidence.

> *Our goal is to create long-term opportunities through movement, education, and community.*

---

### Support Us
Move Zanzibar grows thanks to the support of partners, visitors, and friends around the world. 

There are many ways to support our community:
1.  **Book a performance** for your resort or event.
2.  **Support** our youth training program.
3.  **Contribute** with donations or equipment.
4.  **Connect us** with new opportunities.

*Every contribution goes directly into our community and helps us continue our work with young people in Zanzibar.*
